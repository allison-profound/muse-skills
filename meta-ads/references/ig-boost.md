# Boost an existing Instagram post

- A boost turns one Instagram post the advertiser already published into a campaign, ad set and ad in one step, all paused. It follows the shared stages in [campaign creation](campaign-creation.md), apart from what this guide changes: the post is the creative, so there is no creative stage, and the tool proposes defaults for any setting the advertiser leaves open.

- A boost suits a request that names an Instagram post, such as "boost my latest reel", and a request to promote the business when a recent post already shows the offer. New creative, several audiences or several ads need a full campaign instead.

## Start from what is already settled

- The request and the evidence may already settle the ad account, the Instagram identity, the post, the budget, the duration, the audience or the goal. Work out what is still missing and ask only about that. Don't ask again about a choice the advertiser already made.

- A request for a recommendation stops at the recommended plan, and nothing is created until the advertiser asks for the boost. Show the recommended plan as a preview, so the advertiser can approve exactly what they saw.

## Verify the post

- Verify the ad account, the Instagram identity linked to it and the post the advertiser means, as [identity](campaign-planning.md#resolve-identity) describes. [Audiences and Pages](tool-routing.md#audiences-and-pages) lists the reads that provide them and the IDs each needs.

- The Instagram account linked to the ad account has the advertiser's posts. A missing Instagram connection does not mean there is nothing to boost or recommend.

- Match the post the advertiser described, such as the newest reel for "my latest reel". When several posts fit, let the advertiser choose. When none fits, say what you found and ask.

- When you recommend the post, budget, duration or audience, ground them in the account's recent posts and past boost results when those exist. The tool's defaults are a starting point, not a recommendation.

- Some posts cannot be boosted, for example a reel with licensed music. Say why in plain words and offer a post that can be boosted, or reposting the content without what blocked it.

## Preview and approve the exact plan

- Prepare a preview that creates nothing, with the settings the advertiser gave and the ones you recommend. Settings neither covers come back as the tool's proposed defaults. Show those apart from what the advertiser chose and what you recommended, together with the post, the goal and button and any special ad category.

- Compare the preview with the request and your recommendation before showing it. A default that contradicts either, such as a 6-day duration for "all week" or a nationwide audience after you recommended a local one, needs a corrected preview first, or a clear note that the plan does not match yet.

- Choosing a post settles only the post, including agreeing to a different post after one cannot be boosted. The budget, duration and goal stay open until the advertiser chooses them or accepts the proposed defaults, so a new post gets a new preview and approval.

- An advertiser who gave every setting and asked to create the boost still sees the preview first. Their request settles the values, and the preview shows what will be created from them.

- Any change to the post or a setting gets a new preview. Create only the resolved plan the advertiser approved: every setting they chose or changed, such as a local audience, and any defaults they accepted. A tool default never replaces a setting they approved. [Write operations](writes.md#other-writes) covers the calls.

## After creation

- Check what was created and report only what the result proves, then hand off as [handoff](campaign-handoff.md) describes.

- Start the boost only when the advertiser asks. Keep any limit they set, such as "create it but don't start it", for the rest of the conversation.

- A rejected or uncertain boost follows the retry and verification rules in [write operations](writes.md). A retry repeats the approved settings exactly, apart from the field the error named.
